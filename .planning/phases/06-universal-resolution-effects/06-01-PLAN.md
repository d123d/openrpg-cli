<feature>
  <name>Universal d20 test pipeline</name>
  <files>srd_cli/rules/resolution.py, tests/test_resolution.py, tests/test_resolution_srd_golden.py</files>
  <behavior>
    - Ability check/save/attack: d20 + ability modifier + at most one proficiency contribution + circumstantial modifiers versus DC/AC.
    - Expertise doubles proficiency contribution, not full modifier; inapplicable proficiency/expertise contributes zero.
    - Any advantage source plus any disadvantage source yields one normal d20; otherwise source count still yields at most two d20 and chosen high/low.
    - Natural 20/1 flags are retained; automatic attack hit/miss semantics are typed for caller use, not incorrectly applied to checks or saves.
    - Contest resolves both typed tests with explicit deterministic tie result; passive score is 10 plus same non-roll modifiers with advantage/disadvantage adjustment encoded once.
    - Every roll reports dice, selected natural, modifier breakdown, total, target, outcome, RNG draw span, and SRD rule key.
  </behavior>
